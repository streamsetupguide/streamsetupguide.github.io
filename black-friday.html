Does the referral bonus stack with the BF discount?

Yes — they're different systems. The BF discount comes off at checkout; referral bonus days get added to the plan after purchase. The 2025 deal thread was full of people asking exactly this, and the answer held: discounted purchase + referral days = both apply. (One caveat: verify on the official 2026 thread when it drops — terms can change year to year.)

The hour it drops — checklist

  1. Verify it's real: the official thread appears in TorBox's community spaces (subreddit, Discord, X). Screenshot the terms — especially whether the discount locks for renewal life and whether existing users can cancel-resub.
  2. Do the math for YOUR case: days remaining ÷ plan price you'd pay. If you have months left, stacking an annual at 30% off is usually still the cheapest time you'll buy all year.
  3. Buy once, correctly: discount at checkout (no code expected), confirmation shows the reduced price before you pay.
  4. Confirm the stack: after purchase, your expiry should show old time + new time (+ referral days if it was a fresh signup).
Don't: buy at full price in November "to be safe" — the deal has run two years straight. And don't delete-and-rebuy an existing plan without confirming the 2026 terms allow it.

What about other sales?

TorBox runs occasional promos outside BF (birthday codes, flash discounts — a 20% birthday code ran in September 2026). Useful, but none have matched BF's 30%-off-everything-annual with life-of-subscription locking. If your plan expires before November, a smaller promo beats an outage; if you can wait, BF is the yearly floor.
