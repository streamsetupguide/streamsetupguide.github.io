New episodes, zero clicks: the Trakt → TorBox auto-download pipeline

Add a show to your Trakt watchlist; new episodes appear in your TorBox cloud in the best quality that exists — automatically. This is the full working recipe, including the two gotchas that break everyone's setup.

What you're building

Trakt watchlist (your "want list") → showrss (per-show episode feeds with magnets) → TorBox RSS (auto-download into your cloud) — with a small polling script as the reliability backstop. Result: Tuesday's episode is in your cloud Tuesday night, cached and streamable, without you touching anything.

Step 1 — Trakt as the source of truth

Trakt is just the nicest place to maintain "shows I follow." Any tracker works, but Trakt's watchlist + history (which the pruning step later uses) is the cleanest. Add shows to your watchlist as you discover them — that's the entire manual part of this system.

Step 2 — showrss feeds per show

showrss.info publishes one RSS feed per TV show, with magnet links per episode release. Free, no account needed.

  1. Search your show on showrss, grab its feed URL (looks like showrss.info/show/1234.rss).
  2. Prefer the "full show" feed — it carries every new episode automatically.

Reality check: showrss covers scene-released shows well. Reality/variety shows (especially regional ones) often never appear — those stay manual.

Step 3 — wire it into TorBox

TorBox Pro includes unlimited RSS feeds: dashboard → RSS, add each show's feed. Two settings matter:

  • Filter (do-regex): use the SHOW NAME, nothing fancier. The feed is already show-specific.
  • Quality: feeds carry 2160p/1080p/720p releases of the same episode — decide whether TorBox should take everything (you'll get duplicates in multiple qualities) or you filter in the next step.

⚠️ Gotcha #1 — the filter format trap

showrss titles episodes as Show 2x10 — not Show S02E10. If you write a filter like Show S02E*, it will never match a single episode, while the feed reports "checked successfully." This is the silent killer: everything looks healthy, nothing ever downloads. Filter on the show name only.

Step 4 — the 20-line backstop script

Poll the feeds yourself and add magnets via TorBox's API (free tier of any scheduler works — a cron job, GitHub Action, a Raspberry Pi):

  1. Every few hours, fetch each show'3 showrss feed.
  2. Parse items: title + magnet link.
  3. Pick the best quality per episode (one copy only — 2160p preferred, and skip "AI upscaled" releases: a native 2160p WEB-DL with Dolby Vision beats an upscaled 4320p re-encode every time).
  4. Check your existing torrents (match on season/episode parsed from titles) — skip what you have.
  5. Add new magnets via POST /torrents/createtorrent.

That's genuinely the whole thing. Two extra behaviors worth adding: auto-upgrade (if a better quality of an episode you hold appears later, add it and delete the lesser copy) and a watched-episode pruner (Trakt history → delete watched episodes → keeps your slot count sane).

Why this beats manual adding

  • Episodes are often cached by the time you sit down — instant playback.
  • No more "did I grab this week's episode" — the answer is always yes.
  • Works for seasonal binges: add the show once, get every week's episode for months.
