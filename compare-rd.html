TorBox vs Real-Debrid — the honest comparison

RD is the incumbent most people came from; TorBox is the newer all-in-one. This is a fair look at both — including where RD still wins — written by someone who runs TorBox daily and has watched the RD ecosystem for years.

The 30-second version

Real-Debrid: cheaper entry, the deepest caching history (older catalogs are pre-cached from years of dominance), the widest addon compatibility — every Stremio tool supports it first.

TorBox: one subscription covers torrents and Usenet and web hosters, plus actual cloud features (1 TB storage, S3 access, unlimited RSS, an API people build on). One-time plans instead of auto-renewal.

If you want the cheapest scraper-addon pipe and nothing else: RD. If you want a cloud platform you can build on: TorBox. The gap narrows every year TorBox's cache grows.

Price per day

RD's long-term plans are famously cheap per day. TorBox's list prices are higher — but TorBox sells one-time plans (no auto-renew) and runs deep sales (Black Friday historically 30% off annual), and its BF math page shows how stacking gets effective cost down dramatically. Check both at their current prices — this column flips depending on what promotions are live.

Caching and speed

Both are fast; the difference is catalog depth. RD's cache has more years of downloads behind it, so obscure older torrents are more often pre-cached. TorBox's cache is younger but modern and growing fast — anything recent (current-season TV, new releases) is typically instant on both. For new-release streaming through Stremio addons, users report parity; for decade-old rarities, RD still holds an edge.

What TorBox has that RD doesn't

  • Usenet included — no separate newsreader subscription; TorBox downloads NZBs natively (external indexers for search).
  • Web hoster downloads — supported hosters fetch directly into your cloud.
  • 1 TB AirLock cloud storage with T3 (S3-compatible mount — see the rclone guide) — your files, mountable, streamable as a library.
  • Unlimited RSS feeds — auto-download new episodes of your shows (the pipeline guide).
  • A first-class API — the automation surface is genuinely good; third-party tooling exists beyond addons.
  • One-time plans — buy 6 or 12 months, no subscription to remember to cancel.

What RD has that TorBox doesn't (yet)

  • Deeper cache history on old/rare content.
  • Wider first-party addon support — every scraper addon (Torrentio, Comet, etc.) treats RD as the default debrid; TorBox support is common now but occasionally second-tier.
  • Habit — most tutorials, most communities, most configs assume RD. Changing takes an afternoon.

Who should pick which

  • Pick RD if: you only scrape via Stremio addons, want the lowest sticker price, and watch a lot of old/rare content where its cache wins.
  • Pick TorBox if: you want torrents + Usenet in one bill, any automation (RSS episodes, API scripts, rclone mounts), actual cloud storage, or hate auto-renewing subscriptions.
  • Plenty of people run both during transition months — addons happily take multiple debrids; compare caching on your own library before committing.
